# LinkedIn

Copy to `instructions/linkedin.md` to make it yours. That file is git-ignored.

**Account:** a personal LinkedIn. Professional network, warm audience, people who may
become customers or who already know you.

## Shape

Six short lines maximum. No paragraph blocks. If it does not fit in six lines you
have not found the point yet — cut, do not compress.

The first one or two lines are all that shows before LinkedIn's "see more"
truncation. Assume nobody reads past the hook unless it earns the click.

## What performs here

Specifics beat lessons. A number you actually measured, a decision you actually
made, a thing that actually broke. The reader is scrolling past forty posts of
advice; the only thing that stops them is evidence that a real person did a real
thing this week.

Replace this section with the post of yours that worked best, and tell the model to
keep hitting that shape. One concrete reference post is worth ten rules.

## Specific to LinkedIn

- No outbound links in the body. LinkedIn suppresses reach on posts that send people
  away. If a link matters, it goes in the first comment.
- No hashtag runs. One, or none.
- Line breaks are load-bearing. One idea per line.
- Do not tag people to get their audience. Tag someone only when the post is about
  something they did.

## Engagement

Comment only when you have something specific to add. React otherwise. The people
worth engaging with are the ones posting real numbers or real technical detail, not
the ones posting advice.

## DMs

Only to first-degree connections, and only about something they said or did. A DM
that could have been sent to a thousand people reads exactly like it was.
