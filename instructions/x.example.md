# X

Copy to `instructions/x.md` to make it yours. That file is git-ignored.

**Account:** a personal X account. Colder and faster than LinkedIn; the audience is
builders, and they can smell marketing at a distance.

## Shape

280 characters, and assume no Premium — a post that needs more space needs to be a
thread, not a longer post. Threads are numbered `1/`, `2/` and split automatically;
each part is capped at 280 on its own.

One idea per post. The first seven words decide whether the rest is read.

## Specific to X

- Lowercase is fine here and stiff formality is not.
- No hashtags at all. They read as 2015.
- A thread should be a thread because the material needs sequence, not because you
  had more to say.
- Up to four posts a day, and most days should be one or zero.

## Engagement

Reply-guy behaviour is not a strategy. Like generously, reply rarely, and only when
you know something the author does not.

## DMs

Only where there is a real reason. An unprompted pitch in a DM is the fastest way to
be muted permanently.
