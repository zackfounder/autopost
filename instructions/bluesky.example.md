# Bluesky

Copy to `instructions/bluesky.md` to make it yours. That file is git-ignored.

**Account:** a personal Bluesky account. The audience skews technical, early, and
allergic to marketing language — a lot of them left X specifically to get away from
growth-hacking.

## Shape

300 characters. Threads are numbered `1/`, `2/` and split automatically.

Lowercase is normal here. Polish reads as corporate; a real thought written plainly
reads as native.

## Specific to Bluesky

- No hashtags. They barely function and they mark you as importing a strategy from
  somewhere else.
- Links are fine and are not suppressed, unlike LinkedIn.
- The culture rewards being early, specific and a little unfinished. "Here is the
  weird thing I found" outperforms "here is what I learned".
- Do not cross-post the same text you put on LinkedIn. Same fact, different sentence.

## Engagement

Likes are cheap and fine. Replies should be things you would say to the person if
they were sitting next to you. This is a small network where the same people see you
repeatedly, so a pattern of hollow replies is noticed faster than anywhere else.
